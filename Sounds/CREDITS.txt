The sounds clash_*.ogg / clash_*.wav and the music Music/clash.ogg (re-encoded, from "wild battle (kanto).ogg")
come from the Pokémon SDK technical demo (Pokémon Workshop, https://github.com/PokemonWorkshop), its audio/se and
audio/bgm folders (clash_ball_* : the sounds of the pokeballs). Some are trimmed.

The sounds map_*.wav (the steps of the run, the bump of a wall, the jump and its landing), battle_encounter.wav and
bond_*.ogg (the bond cinematic) come from the same demo, its audio/se folder ("se_step_run_dirt", "se_step_road",
"player bump", "se_jump", "se_jump (landing)", "se_gui_battle_ready", "in-battle_ability_activate", "136-light02",
"se_shiny", "141-burst01").

The sounds battle_stat_up.wav and battle_stat_down.wav (a statistic rises, falls in a battle) are made by
tools/assets/battle_sounds.py (sine waves, no sample).
